# Domain Glossary

## Platform

The complete We Move ecosystem.

## Core

The shared foundation used by all products.

## Product

A specific application built on the platform.

## Company

An organization using We Move Platform.

## User

A person with access to the platform.

## Tenant

A logical boundary for isolating company data.

## Lead

A potential customer or opportunity.

## Service Order

A structured unit of work related to a customer service.

## Booking

A scheduled meeting or appointment.

## Network

The community and social layer of the platform.
