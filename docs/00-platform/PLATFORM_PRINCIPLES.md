# PLATFORM_PRINCIPLES

## The Constitution of We Move Platform

**Architecture Version:** 1.0 – Foundation

### Preamble

We Move Platform is not built for the next sprint.

It is built for the next decade.

Every architectural decision should make the platform more coherent, maintainable and valuable.

When in doubt, return to this document.

---

## Principle 0

**The Platform is more important than any individual product.**

---

## Principle 1

**The Domain comes before the Technology.**

---

## Principle 2

**Behavior comes before implementation.**

Expected behavior → Test → Code.

---

## Principle 3

**Every important decision deserves documentation.**

---

## Principle 4

**Names are architecture.**

Protect the Ubiquitous Language.

---

## Principle 5

**The Core belongs to everyone, not to a single product.**

---

## Principle 6

**Architecture evolves intentionally.**

Major changes require:
- ADR
- Tests
- Documentation

---

## Principle 7

**Prefer simplicity over cleverness.**

---

## Principle 8

**Every abstraction must justify its existence.**

---

## Principle 9

**TDD is the default way of building software.**

---

## Principle 10

**The code should read like the business.**

---

## Principle 11

**Documentation is part of the product.**

---

## Principle 12

**We optimize for longevity, not shortcuts.**

---

## The Architect's Rule

If someone says:

> "Let's do it quickly."

Someone else must ask:

> "How will this decision affect We Move Platform in five years?"

---

## The Final Question

Does this decision make We Move Platform better?
